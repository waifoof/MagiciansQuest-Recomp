Magician's Quest: Mysterious Times, recompiled for PC. No game data is included: choose your own ROM (USA) on the
start screen and the game is built from it once.

**Getting started:** extract the zip somewhere you can write to and run `MagiciansQuest.exe`. Pick the **Enhanced**
preset for the recommended setup (one 16:9 picture, 60 fps, smoothed text and textures). F1 opens the settings.

**What's in it**

- **One screen (16:9):** the DS's two screens are two halves of one view, so they are joined into one wide picture.
  Where only the bottom screen has 3D (classrooms, shops, rooms) it fills the window, with the top screen in a corner
  (F3 or the right stick: small, large, hidden). Menus show both screens side by side. Empty far sides that the game
  never built are filled from the scene beside them (Settings > Video > Fill empty edges).
- **Zoom (widescreen):** where one screen fills the window (rooms, shops, classrooms), the camera comes in just
  enough that the picture is filled with what the game really draws, not empty sides (Settings > Video > Zoom).
- **PC view (in testing, Settings > Video > Layout: PC view, OpenGL):** one picture of the world from one camera
  instead of two DS screens: the town as one wide view with the sky above (one continuous scene: the near walls,
  planters and ledges now carry on past where the two DS screens met, with no line across the picture, under one
  fog, and no tear there while the camera moves: both parts are always of the same moment, at 60 fps; the sky's colour bands reach the window's edges, in town and in the beach, forest and Lovers Point; the forest by Charlie's Noodles no longer shows black wedges at the wide sides (the trees fade out softly); no band in a student's hair
  colours running to the window's edge when someone stands at the side of the view, as while fishing by the bookshop), rooms and shops filling the window, the
  bag and hand in the window's corners (only while the game shows them: not while fishing), the text box (with its choices and lists, however tall) at a sharp
  whole-number scale at the bottom (and so are menus with no text box under them, such as the post office's "Other
  player / Classmate / Exit", clickable there), and menus (the bag, the Start menu's pages: map, info, telegrams,
  friends, magic; the shops' catalogues and lists: Harrows' CD orders, The Emporium's sell list, Mon Paris'
  hairstyles; the telegram's recipients, the bell's composer, character creation) as a centred panel of both screens
  over the scene from before them (no longer with a curtain or a text box caught in it). While the game's keyboard is
  up (your name at enrolment, a telegram's text) you can type on your PC's keyboard: letters, digits, space,
  Backspace, Enter for OK. At the school bell's composer, your keyboard's home row plays its piano. Saving's "Please wait..." box shows
  alone where the text box is; your chanted words appear above your student; a click on the world (an insect with
  the net, a classmate) goes where you click, across the whole wide picture; the tournament's word panel sits over
  the auditorium; speech balloons beside the bag stay where they are. The scene dimmed behind a menu no longer shows the bag and hand icons;
  rooms' far sides past the room's own walls (the bookshop) fade softly into the dark instead of smeared streaks; a
  click just beside a speech balloon or a label goes to the world, not to it.
- **60 fps 3D on both screens**, high resolution (up to 16x), widescreen (16:9 to 32:9) in every layout.
- **Smooth 2D:** text, menus and sprites upscaled with smooth diagonals instead of blocks.
- **Upscale textures:** the game's textures enlarged 4x with a pixel-art filter (crisp edges, smooth diagonals).
- **Texture packs:** Settings > Video > Dump textures saves the game's textures to `textures\dump` in the data
  folder; put edited or redrawn ones (1, 2 or 4 times the size, same names) in `textures\load`.
- **Walk speed** (only your character), **text speed**, **quick transitions**, **fast forward** (hold Tab, or View + RB on a pad; not while
  playing together, where both games keep the same speed: a press says so). Fast and
  instant text now speed up the game's own printing, so what a line does at its end still happens when you press A
  early (a purchase at Daisy's or a drink at the café is paid: before, the item was given and no Ritch taken).
- **Your PC's clock** is the game's: days, hours and seasons pass with real time.
- **Pause in background** (the game waits while you're in another window; not while connected to another player),
  and the mouse pointer hides while you play.
- **The window remembers its place:** its size and position (and whether it was maximized) when you close the game
  come back the next time, as long as that is still on a screen.
- **Co-op notices:** in the corner, when your partner's game stops answering (closed, or the connection dropped) and
  when it is back, and once if the round trip stays over 350 ms (the window's title says the same, but it is not seen
  fullscreen, as on a Steam Deck). Settings > Network > Co-op status (off by default) keeps a small line in the
  top-left corner: "Partner: Café · 210 ms" (where your partner is, when their game says so: every shop and building
  of the town, Lovers Point, the beach, the cave, Fungi Forest and more), "Partner: not answering". The window's
  title says it in plain words too: "Co-op: hosting, waiting for your partner" / "partner connected", and over the
  internet the ping and "playing together".
- **Fast forward and screenshot on a controller** (a Steam Deck has no spare button): hold View and press RB (fast
  forward, held) or LB (screenshot); View alone is still Select. Remappable in the Controls window, where a row
  takes a pair of buttons too.

  from the first day. Settings > Network > Co-op between PCs: one player Hosts, the others Join (the host is found by
  itself on the same network; over the internet, type its address: the host's game opens UDP port 49251 on its router
  by itself, else forward it by hand). Start your
  games, go upstairs to the Gateway in the dorm, communications room, Multi-Card Play: the host picks "Come and play
  with me!", the others "Can we play together?". While connected, the first day's holds are lifted, so you can walk

  the introduction to the class, the wand and hat), which ends the first day in each player's own save; the
  principal and teachers are there while you're connected, so you can take classes together. The café, the
  insect and fish books and the shops' CDs, part-time work and berry seedlings are open to visitors too. Two copies of the game on one PC find each other too. Details in
  docs/COOP.md.
